For a long time I kept my notes in a folder no one would ever open. They were honest in the way private things are honest, and useless in the way private things are useless. Nothing pushed back. Nothing made me finish a sentence.

So I built a place to put the finished sentences.

## Why a site and not a feed

Feeds reward the reflex and punish the second thought. You post, the numbers move, and the idea is gone before it had to defend itself. A page is slower. It sits at one address. People can link to it, argue with it, return to it a year later and find it unchanged. That permanence asks more of the writing, and asking more is the whole point.

> The best reason to publish is that it forces you to mean what you say.

I am not trying to reach everyone. I am trying to reach the handful of people who will read carefully, and the version of me who shows up next year looking for what I actually thought.

## What goes here

Two streams. Longer essays, which I will revise until they hold together. And shorter notes, which I will not. The notes are closer to the old private folder, just with the door propped open.

If something here is wrong, it is wrong in public now, which means I am more likely to fix it. That is the trade I wanted.
